Privacy Policy

Offline AI Notepad · Last updated 27 September 2026

In one sentence: Offline AI Notepad collects nothing, sends nothing and makes no network connections — your notes, photos and everything the AI does with them stay on your device.

What the app stores

WhatWhereWho can see it
Your notes, their titles, folders and last five versionsA file in the app's own storage on your device, with a backup copy beside it, protected by iOS file encryptionOnly you
Photos you add to notesShrunk copies, as image files in the app's own storage on your deviceOnly you
Preferences (theme, text size, sort order, folder names)The app's settings on your deviceOnly you
Ask conversationsMemory only — cleared when the app closesOnly you

The AI

The language model is bundled inside the app and runs on your device's processor. Your notes are given to it on the device and nowhere else. There is no server, no cloud model and no download. Photos are never given to the model.

Your photo library

The app has no access to your photo library. When you add a photo, iOS shows its own picker and the app receives only the photo you choose. A shrunk copy is kept with the note, on the device.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or sync.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No network connections of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or calendar.

Sharing

If you use Share, Copy or Export, the text goes where you send it, through iOS. The app does not see what happens after that.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note removes it (with a brief Undo); photos no note uses any more are deleted the next time the app starts. Deleting a folder never deletes its notes. Deleting the app removes every note, photo, version and setting it stored. Nothing exists anywhere else to delete.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change with it.
